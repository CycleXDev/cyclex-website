Frequently Asked Questions

Is this safe?
Yes. The scan is completely read-only. CycleX never asks for your seed phrase, never takes custody, and never moves your funds. You only connect your wallet if you choose to revoke an approval.
Do I need to connect my wallet?
No. You can paste any public wallet address to scan. Connection is only required when you choose to revoke an approval — and only the wallet that was scanned can revoke its own permissions.
What does "Revoke" mean?
Revoke removes a permission (approval) that you previously granted to a spender contract. It calls approve(spender, 0) on-chain, which sets the allowance to zero. It does not sell, transfer or move your assets.
Does a high score mean I was hacked?
No. A high risk score means your wallet has active permissions or exposure signals that deserve review. Many approvals are from legitimate DeFi interactions — the score helps you prioritise which ones to check first.
What is the Free Revoke?
Every wallet gets one free revoke — for the single highest-risk permission detected. This removes your biggest active threat at no cost. Full cleanup of all approvals requires a CycleX PASS.
Can communities use this?
Yes. CycleX creates branded safety pages for wallets, DeFi apps and Web3 communities. Request a partner page and your users get the same powerful tools under your community's name, powered by CycleX.
